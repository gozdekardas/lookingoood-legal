Lookingoood Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Lookingoood app collects, how it is used and protected, and your rights under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Data Controller

Lookingoood is developed by an individual developer, who is the data controller. Contact: lookingooodapp@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

Account information

  • Email address and password, handled by Firebase Authentication. Your email is never shown to other users.
  • Username and full name.

Profile

  • Profile photo and bio.

Content you create

  • Posts: outfit photos, canvas images, captions, categories and product tags (name, brand, price and shopping link).
  • Wardrobe items: photos, category, brand, price and shopping link.
  • Looks: outfit collages with their pieces, and whether each look is public or private.
  • Comments, including @mentions of other users.
  • Activity: who you follow, posts you hang (save), and ratings you give to other users' public looks.
  • Safety: users you block and content you report.

Technical data

  • Crash reports from Firebase Crashlytics (device model, iOS version, app state and technical details at the time of a crash), used only to fix bugs.
  • App integrity checks through Firebase App Check and Apple's App Attest, which confirm that requests come from the genuine app.
  • Technical records kept by our service providers to run and secure the service, such as IP addresses in server logs, and counters used to limit spam.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising services.

3. Data That Stays on Your Device

  • You choose photos with the system photo picker; the app only receives the photos you select.
  • Background removal for wardrobe items is done on your device. Photos are not sent to any other service for this.
  • Post drafts are saved only on your device and are never uploaded until you post them. They are removed if you delete the app or your account.

4. What Other Users Can See

  • Visible to other users: your username, full name, profile photo and bio; your posts and their tags; your comments; your public looks and their average rating; your follower and following counts; and who hung your posts (shown to you as the post owner).
  • Only visible to you: your email, wardrobe, private looks and drafts.
  • Not shown to other users in the app: the list of users you blocked.
  • When you follow, hang, comment, mention or rate, the other user receives an in-app notification showing your username and profile photo.

5. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To provide the app's features: wardrobe, looks, posts, feed, comments, ratings and notifications.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, enforcing blocks and limiting spam.
  • To fix crashes and technical problems.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

6. Service Providers and Sharing

  • Google LLC / Firebase provides authentication, database, file storage, server functions, crash reporting and app integrity checks.
  • Apple provides App Attest to verify that the app is genuine.
  • We do not sell your personal data or share it with advertisers.
  • Shopping links in tags open third-party websites. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

7. International Transfer

Firebase stores and processes data on servers in the United States. By using the app you consent to this transfer under Article 9 of KVKK.

8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

  • Your data is kept while your account is active.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile > ··· > Delete Account.
  • Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, posts, wardrobe, looks, comments, hangs, ratings, the notifications you sent, and all images you uploaded. This happens immediately, and in any case within 30 days.
  • Reports you submitted or that concern you may be kept as long as necessary for safety and legal reasons.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.

9. Your Rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed, and request information about it;
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose;
  • learn which third parties your data is transferred to, in Turkey or abroad;
  • request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of your data under Article 7 of KVKK;
  • object to results that arise from automated analysis of your data;
  • claim compensation for damages caused by unlawful processing.

Send requests to lookingooodapp@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children

Lookingoood is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page, with the date it was last updated.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Privacy Policy.

The data controller responsible for your personal data is the individual developer of Lookingoood, reachable at:

Email: lookingooodapp@gmail.com

Your data is shared only with the following service provider:

Due to Firebase infrastructure, your personal data is stored on servers located in the United States. By using the App, you consent to this transfer as required under KVKK Article 9.

Your personal data is retained for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

Under KVKK Article 11, you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed
  • Request information about how it is processed
  • Learn the third parties your data has been transferred to
  • Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (KVKK Article 7)

To exercise these rights, email us at lookingooodapp@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we become aware of such data, it will be deleted immediately.

We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification or email. The current version is always available on this page.
